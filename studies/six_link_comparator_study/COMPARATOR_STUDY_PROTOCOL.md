# Independent Comparator Study Protocol

## Status

Prepared after inspection of the current manuscript results. Not externally
timestamped, not yet executed, and not part of the present paper's evidence.

## Question

When independent reviewers inspect the same benchmark-claim evidence packets,
does the six-link worked template change defect detection, claim-strength
classification, inter-rater agreement, completion time, or perceived burden
relative to a compact conventional checklist?

## Design

- Parallel, randomized, blinded-label comparison.
- Target sample: at least 12 independent raters with prior experience reviewing
  machine-learning, PHM, reliability, or reproducibility studies.
- Each rater receives six evidence packets, balanced by a Latin-square
  assignment across template arm and packet order.
- The two instruments contain comparable prompts. The six-link arm names the
  estimand/unit, design status, evidence role, artifact, completed-choice
  behavior, and permitted wording. The baseline arm asks for claim, method,
  result, reproducibility, and limitation checks without the six-link labels.
- Packets contain deliberately seeded and naturally occurring traceability
  defects. The hidden gold key is frozen before recruitment.

## Outcomes

Primary descriptive outcomes are defect-detection sensitivity against the
frozen gold key, false-positive defect rate, ordinal claim-strength agreement,
inter-rater agreement within each arm, and completion time per packet.
Secondary outcome is a five-item burden score. No universal efficacy or
superiority claim is permitted from this single study.

## Analysis unit and estimands

The rater is the independent unit. Packet-level responses are repeated
measurements. Report arm differences with rater-clustered descriptive
uncertainty and raw rater-level paired or balanced effects. Do not treat
individual checklist items as independent samples.

## Exclusions and missingness

Eligibility and exclusion rules must be applied before outcome access. Missing
packet responses remain missing; no last-observation or item-mean imputation is
allowed. Timing outliers are retained and accompanied by median sensitivity.

## Governance

Human-participant or expert-elicitation requirements must be checked before
recruitment. The current author may prepare materials but may not count as an
independent rater. Generative-AI reviewers do not satisfy the independent-rater
criterion and, if explored separately, must be reported as a distinct
algorithmic study.
