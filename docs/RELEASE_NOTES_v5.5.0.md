## Evidence boundary

The C-MAPSS analysis is retrospective. Formal execution began before the analysis-plan freeze; the freeze preceded complete-result aggregation. It is a pre-aggregation lock, not preregistration. The battery protocol was frozen before training and test-label evaluation. The results do not establish a universal model winner, physical sensor-fault robustness, field maintenance benefit, or an optimized maintenance policy.

The complete observed-prefix policy replay was not independently completed within the local 10-minute audit window. Stored outputs are included in the OSF archive and their status is not described as an independent replay.
